The Sentinel Streaming binary is distributed under the repository license in
`LICENSE`.

MediaMTX is not downloaded or automatically redistributed by the default
bundle. If an operator supplies a MediaMTX executable with `MEDIAMTX_BIN`, the
operator is responsible for retaining the applicable MediaMTX license and
notices alongside that runtime. MediaMTX remains a separate third-party
component and is not Sentinel-owned code.

FFmpeg is an external runtime used by selected source and clip-capture paths;
it is not bundled by this package. Operators must obtain and comply with the
license terms of their FFmpeg distribution.
